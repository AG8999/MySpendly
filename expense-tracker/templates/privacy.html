Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. When you use Spendly to track your finances, we collect the expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect limited technical information, such as your browser type and general usage patterns, to help us maintain and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide to operate the Service, including displaying your expenses, generating spending summaries and category breakdowns, and authenticating your account. We may also use aggregated, non-identifying usage information to understand how the Service is used and to guide future improvements. We do not use your personal expense data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time, after which we will remove it from our active systems.

4. Third Party Services

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. We may rely on trusted third-party providers for functions such as hosting, and these providers only process data as needed to deliver those services on our behalf. We do not share your expense data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided within the app, and we will be happy to assist you.
